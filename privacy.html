Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Geraldine Berry Barn Bakery & Cafe respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it. This is placeholder wording for the demo and should be reviewed before going live.

1. What we collect

When you contact us through this website, we collect only what you give us — typically your name, email, phone number and the details of your enquiry or feedback.

2. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, catering request or feedback
  • To confirm and arrange any order you ask about
  • To improve our cafe and the service we offer

We don't sell or rent your information to anyone.

3. Form submissions

Our contact and feedback forms are handled by FormSubmit, which delivers your message to our email inbox. Your details are used only to respond to you.

4. Reviews

If you choose to leave a public review, that's handled by Google under their own terms and privacy policy. Private feedback you send us stays with us.

5. Cookies and analytics

This site may use basic cookies or analytics to understand how visitors use it. You can disable cookies in your browser at any time.

6. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020, you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and request a correction. Just get in touch.
