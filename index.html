About the Barn

A bakery cafe right in the middle of Geraldine

The Berry Barn sits in Four Peaks Plaza on Talbot Street, beside the Geraldine Cinema. Everything in the cabinet is baked on site early each morning, so by 7am the pies are hot and the coffee's on.

It's a regular for locals and a handy stop for anyone heading between Christchurch, Tekapo and Queenstown. Grab a table inside, sit out front, or take it for the road. Keep an eye out for the bees: there's a hive on site.

  • Baked on site every morning
  • Gluten-free options in the cabinet
  • Dine in, outdoor tables or takeaway

Pull in off Talbot Street

Drive 4 Hearts pulled in at the Barn

Drive 4 Hearts is New Zealand's oldest Land Rover on a mission. The 1948 Series I was the 20th Land Rover ever built and the first to arrive in New Zealand. It drove from Dunedin to Auckland raising money for Manaaki Manawa, the University of Auckland's Centre for Heart Research, to develop a heart valve that grows with children's hearts.

When it came through Geraldine it parked up right outside the Barn, with Land Rovers lined up across Four Peaks Plaza and the bunting out.

Good to know

What are your opening hours?

7am to 2:30pm every day, with cooked breakfasts until 1pm. If you're planning around a public holiday, give us a ring on 03 693 9900 to check.

Do you have gluten-free food?

Yes, including gluten-free pies. What's in the cabinet changes day to day, so ask at the counter what's on.

Where are you?

76 Talbot Street, Geraldine, in Four Peaks Plaza beside the Geraldine Cinema. It's the main road through town, so it's an easy stop if you're driving through.

Can we sit in, or is it takeaway only?

Both. There are tables inside and out the front, or we'll pack it up for the road.

Can you do a bigger order for a group?

Give us a call on 03 693 9900 or send an email with what you're after and when, and we'll talk it through with you.

Come and see us

Pop in any day from 7am. For anything else, give us a call or send an email.
