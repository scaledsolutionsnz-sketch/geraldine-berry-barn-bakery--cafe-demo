Our story

A berry barn on the edge of Geraldine

We started out selling berries by the punnet at the gate. These days the barn's a proper bakery and cafe — but the idea hasn't changed. Pick the fruit, bake it the same day, and put on a coffee good enough that people plan their drive around it.

Whether you're heading over to the lakes, coming back from the coast, or you're a local after your usual, there's always something warm in the cabinet and a friendly face behind the counter.

  • Locally owned and run, right here in Geraldine
  • Baking made on site daily — nothing trucked in frozen
  • Berries grown on our canes and by Canterbury growers
